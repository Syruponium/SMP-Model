## Residual Analysis & Environmental Factors
While the base model predicts $R \propto \sqrt{L}$, local environmental factors act as secondary variables:

### Negative Residuals (Friction begins earlier than predicted)
- **NGC 2976:** Part of the M81 group. Tidal interactions have stripped outer gas and "collapsed" the thermal profile. The Matrix cools faster here, leading to an earlier "Snap".
- **Draco/Sculptor:** Satellite galaxies in the cold halo of the Milky Way. The lower background temperature of their environment forces the 13.84 K threshold closer to their centers.

### Positive Residuals (Friction begins later than predicted)
- **DDO 154:** An isolated "Dark Galaxy". It possesses high amounts of HI gas that contributes to the total energy density of the medium but isn't fully accounted for in visual luminosity alone.
- **NGC 3741:** High thermal output relative to its size extends the superfluid zone further out.

The data suggests that "Dark Matter" is not an ingredient, but a **state of the medium**. By observing the Bolometric Luminosity and local environment, galactic rotation can be predicted without free parameters or unobservable particles.
