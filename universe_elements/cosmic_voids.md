# Cosmic Voids: The Viscous Basins of the Matrix

## 1. The Definition of "Empty" Space

In standard cosmology, Voids are simply regions where gravity was too weak to pull matter in. In the SMP model, Voids are **Active Exclusion Zones**.

-   **The State:** Voids represent the Matrix at its lowest temperature and highest viscosity.
    
-   **The Physics:** Far from the thermal output ($L_{bol}$) of galaxies, the Matrix stays well below the **13.84 K Snap**. This makes the "Stroop" (the syrup) at its thickest and most resistant to movement.
    

## 2. The "Push" Mechanism (Anti-Gravity Illusion)

We often observe that Voids seem to "expand" or push galaxies away. SMP explains this through **Hydraulic Displacement**:

-   Because the Matrix in a Void is cold and viscous, it exerts a massive, uniform mechanical pressure ($10^{34}$ Pa).
    
-   Matter (gas and plasma) is naturally "squeezed" out of these high-viscosity regions toward the warmer, superfluid **Filaments**.
    
-   **The Result:** Galaxies don't just "drift" away from Voids; they are mechanically repelled by the thickening of the Matrix.
    

## 3. Dark Energy as Void Pressure

The phenomenon attributed to "Dark Energy"—the accelerated expansion of the universe—is most dominant in the Voids.

-   **SMP Explanation:** Dark Energy is the **Internal Floor Pressure** of the Matrix trying to reach equilibrium.
    
-   As the universe cools, more regions drop below the 13.84 K threshold. The transition from superfluid to viscous creates a "stiffening" of the fabric of space, which we perceive as an outward expansion.
    

## 4. The Shape of the Web

The geometry of the universe (the "Cosmic Web") is a direct result of the battle between **Void Viscosity** and **Galactic Heat**:

1.  **Heat (Galaxies):** Creates superfluid bubbles where matter can clump.
    
2.  **Cold (Voids):** Creates viscous barriers that matter cannot easily penetrate.
    
3.  **Filaments:** The "coastlines" where these two states meet, allowing matter to flow between thermal nodes.
    

## 5. Summary: The Great Regulators

-   **CDM View:** Voids are accidental gaps in the distribution of dark matter.
    
-   **SMP View:** Voids are the "refrigerated" regions of the Matrix that dictate the flow of all baryonic matter via mechanical resistance.
    
-   **Key Inflow:** Voids act as the "compressors" that keep the filaments tight and the galaxies organized.
