# The Big Bang: From Supercritical State to Superfluid Expansion

## 1. Pre-Expansion: The Supercritical Matrix

In the SMP model, the "Big Bang" was not an explosion of matter from "nothing." Instead, it was a **phase transition** of the Matrix itself as a superfluid.

Before $T=0$, the medium existed in a **Supercritical State**:

-   **Initial Temperature ($T_{start}$):** $\approx 10^{30}$ K (just below the Planck temperature).
    
-   **Initial Pressure ($P_{start}$):** Infinite hydraulic tension.
    
-   **State:** At this energy density, the distinction between liquid and gas vanishes. The Matrix was a singular, undifferentiated, and ultra-dense "plasma" of pure potential energy.
    

## 2. The Trigger: Symmetry Breaking

The expansion began when the temperature dropped just enough to cross a critical symmetry-breaking threshold. This caused the Matrix to "flash-evaporate" into a superfluid state, leading to a massive increase in volume—what conventional physics calls **Inflation**.

## 3. The Creation of the "Triangle"

During the first microseconds of expansion, the three fundamental anchors of our universe were "frozen" into the medium:

1.  **Hydraulic Setting ($P_m = 10^{34}$ Pa):** As the medium expanded, it reached a stable equilibrium pressure. This pressure acts as the "stiffness" of space, determining how fast waves (light) can travel.
    
2.  **Wave Speed ($c = 2.99 \times 10^8$ m/s):** The speed of light was defined the moment the Matrix reached its $10^{34}$ Pa floor. $c$ is essentially the speed of sound within the Matrix.
    
3.  **The Thermal Snap ($13.84$ K):** This represents the temperature where the Matrix can finally interact with the first baryonic particles (Hydrogen).
    

## 4. The Acoustic Afterglow (CMB)

What we observe today as the **Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB)** is the acoustic "ringing" of this initial phase transition. In the SMP model:

-   The CMB is not just "old light."
    
-   It is the residual **sonic vibration** of the Matrix as it settled from its supercritical state into a superfluid state.
    
-   The "fluctuations" in the CMB are density ripples (phonons) in the fluid that later became the seeds for the first galaxies.
    

## 5. From Heat to Structure

As the expansion continued, the universe cooled.

-   Regions that cooled fastest became the first **Voids** (high viscosity).
    
-   Regions that stayed warm due to initial density became the **Filaments** (superfluid paths) where matter could flow and eventually gather to form stars.
