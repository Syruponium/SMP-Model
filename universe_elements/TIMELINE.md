# The SMP Universe: A Chronological Overview

This timeline tracks the evolution of the cosmos through the lens of the **Syruponium Matrix Protocol (SMP)**. It moves from the high-energy supercritical phase to the complex, structured universe we observe today.

----------

## Phase 1: The Matrix Flash (T = 0)

**Document:** [`big_bang.md`](https://github.com/Syruponium/SMP-Model/blob/main/universe_elements/big_bang.md)

The universe began as a **Supercritical Fluid**. Through a massive phase transition, the Matrix stabilized its internal floor pressure at **$10^{34}$ Pa**, defining the speed of light ($c$) and setting the stage for everything to follow.

## Phase 2: The Thermal Relief (T + 10⁻³²s to 100 My)

**Document:** [`black_holes_quasars.md`](https://github.com/Syruponium/SMP-Model/blob/main/universe_elements/black_holes_quasars.md)

To protect the $10^{34}$ Pa pressure from overheating, the Matrix created **Hydraulic Relief Valves** (Black Holes). These valves acted as "cosmic air conditioners," venting excess entropy and stabilizing the medium.

## Phase 3: The Particle Factory (T + 100 My to 300 My)

**Document:** [`quasars_and_filaments.md`](https://github.com/Syruponium/SMP-Model/blob/main/universe_elements/quasars_and_filaments.md)

Boundary friction around Quasars condensed raw Matrix energy into high-velocity plasma. As this plasma traveled through **Superfluid Filaments**, it cooled and resonated with the Matrix to form the first **Neutral Hydrogen**.

## Phase 4: The Great Distribution (T + 300 My to 1 Gy)

**Document:** [`cosmic_voids.md`](https://github.com/Syruponium/SMP-Model/blob/main/universe_elements/cosmic_voids.md)

The cooling Matrix split into two states:

-   **Viscous Voids:** Cold regions that mechanically "push" matter away.
    
-   **Superfluid Filaments:** Warm lanes where matter flows freely toward galactic nodes.
    

## Phase 5: The Era of Vortices (T + 1 Gy to Present)

**Document:** [`galaxies_formation.md`](https://github.com/Syruponium/SMP-Model/blob/main/universe_elements/galaxies_formation.md)

Matter gathered at the nodes of the filaments, spinning into massive thermal vortices. The interaction between galactic heat ($L_{bol}$) and the **13.84 K Phase Boundary** created the stable, rotating disks we call galaxies.

## Phase 6: The Modern Observation

**Document:** [`gravitational_lensing.md`](https://github.com/Syruponium/SMP-Model/blob/main/universe_elements/gravitational_lensing.md)

Today, we observe the Matrix's influence through its refractive index. What we once called "Dark Matter" is revealed to be the **mechanical drag and optical refraction** of a pressurized fluid medium.
